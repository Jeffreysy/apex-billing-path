How it breaks

Four systems. Three handoffs. Revenue leaks at every one.

No single system is wrong. The money goes missing in the space between them, where no report looks.

  1. 01 · Case management

    The matter

    MyCase or Filevine knows who the client is and where the case stands.

    Where it breaksA matter closes or changes status and billing never hears about it.
  2. 02 · Contracts

    The agreement

    The fee agreement sets the price, the plan and the payment dates.

    Where it breaksSigned plans that never become a schedule of charges.
  3. 03 · Payments

    The money

    LawPay takes the card, the transfer or the deposit.

    Where it breaksDeclined cards and unmatched deposits that nobody owns.
  4. 04 · Collections

    The follow-up

    Your team calls, takes commitments and escalates.

    Where it breaksCollectors chase a list that was out of date before they started.

How it works

Diagnose once. Then keep it fixed.
